## 3. Description of Service & AI Tiers

Brightcraft is a local-first business workspace for capturing ideas, planning strategy, tracking execution, and using on-device multimodal AI:

- **Free Tier:** Access to workspace planning tools and Free on-device AI (`Apple Foundation Model` on supported devices and LiquidAI `LFM2.5-VL 450M` via `llama.cpp` on supported hardware).
- **Pro Tier (`Brightcraft Pro`):** Paid auto-renewable subscription unlocking the larger Pro local multimodal reasoning model (LiquidAI `LFM3-VL 2B` via `llama.cpp` + `libmtmd`) and Pro workspace features as described on the in-app Subscription screen.
- **No Weekly Token/Credit Caps:** Both Free and Pro models run locally on your device. Hardware requirements (minimum RAM/storage and iOS version) apply to downloading and running local GGUF models.
