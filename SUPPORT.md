1. **Contact Support from Brightcraft:** Open **Settings → Support & Legal → Contact Support** to launch Apple Mail pre-addressed to **`Nitiphoom.dev@gmail.com`** with a topic & diagnostics template.
2. **Report a Bug from Brightcraft:** Open **Settings → Support & Legal → Report a Bug** to launch Apple Mail pre-addressed with reproduction steps and non-sensitive diagnostics (iOS version, app version/build, active AI model, subscription tier).
3. **Direct Email:** Send an email directly to **[Nitiphoom.dev@gmail.com](mailto:Nitiphoom.dev@gmail.com)** if Apple Mail is not configured on your device.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Which AI models run on my device?

- **Free Tier:**
  - **Apple Foundation Model:** Built-in on Apple Intelligence-compatible devices (iPhone 15 Pro / M-series iPad and newer with Apple Intelligence enabled). Zero extra download required.
  - **LiquidAI LFM2.5-VL 450M (Q8_0 via `llama.cpp`):** ~482 MB download. Supports every supported device with ≥ 3.5 GB RAM, including older iPhones without Apple Intelligence, for private local chat and image analysis.
- **Pro Tier (`Brightcraft Pro`):**
  - **LiquidAI LFM3-VL 2B (`llama.cpp` + `libmtmd` Metal):** ~1.25 GB download. Unlocks advanced multimodal vision/OCR and `<think>` step-by-step reasoning across all supported hardware.

To switch or remove a downloaded model: go to **Settings → Capabilities → AI Model**. To delete a downloaded model and reclaim storage, long-press the downloaded row and tap **Delete**.

### 2. Why did a model download fail or pause?

- Ensure you have sufficient free device storage (at least 2× the model download size is recommended during installation) and an active Wi-Fi or cellular connection.
- Downloads can be canceled and resumed safely from **Settings → Capabilities → AI Model**.

### 3. How do I manage, cancel, or restore my Pro subscription?

- **Restore on a new device:** Open **Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases** (or tap **Restore Purchases** on the Upgrade sheet).
- **Cancel or switch plans:** Open **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription**, or go to **iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Brightcraft**.
- **Refunds:** App Store purchases are billed by Apple. Request refunds via [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### 4. How does iCloud Sync work?

- Turn sync on or off anytime under **Settings → iCloud Sync**.
- Brightcraft stores synced data exclusively in your **private iCloud database** tied to your Apple Account. Turning sync off never deletes your local workspaces.

### 5. How do I configure a custom SearXNG search server?

- Go to **Settings → Capabilities → SearXNG** and enter your HTTPS base URL (for example `https://search.example.com`). Only live HTTPS instances are accepted.

### 6. How do I delete my account?

- Go to **Settings → Apple Account → Delete Account**. See [ACCOUNT-DELETION.md](https://github.com/VM451/BrightCraft-Legal/blob/main/ACCOUNT-DELETION.md) for full details.
